what are snakes?

Snakes are long, legless reptiles belonging to the suborder Serpentes.
        They are cold-blooded vertebrates covered in scales, and they eat other animals.
        They live all over the world in forests, deserts, and water

how do snakes hunt?

Snakes use various methods to hunt, including constriction, 
        venom, and ambush tactics. And they are highly adapted for 
        their environments.

why are snakes considered great hunters?

Snakes are great hunters due to their specialized adaptations, such as 
        heat-sensing organs, camouflage, and the ability to swallow prey 
        larger than their head.
